Refund & Payment Support

Last updated: 31 October 2026. This policy explains support and refund requests for Career Buddy digital services. It does not limit mandatory consumer rights.

Current paid services

Premium Career Assessment is ₹499, Intro Coaching ₹149, Standard Refill ₹249 and Extended Refill ₹399, before eligible coupons. Current amounts and terms are confirmed at checkout and may change for future purchases.

Full-discount coupons

When an approved 100% coupon reduces the payable amount to zero, no amount is charged and there is no monetary payment to refund.

Access not activated after payment

Contact support from your registered email with the Razorpay order/payment reference. We will investigate the verified transaction and attempt to restore access or resolve the payment issue appropriately.

Duplicate or incorrect charges

Contact support promptly, providing the relevant payment reference. Eligibility for refunds depends on transaction verification, whether the digital service was delivered or used, the purchase terms and applicable law. An approved refund is limited to the amount actually paid.

Digital assessment and coaching services

Report generation and coaching may begin as soon as access is activated. If there is a service failure or other concern, contact support for review; the availability of a refund will be evaluated case by case under applicable rights and the purchase terms.

How to request assistance

Email info@aiearlycareerguide.com with your registered email, relevant purchase reference and concise explanation. Never send card numbers, CVVs, passwords or verification codes.
