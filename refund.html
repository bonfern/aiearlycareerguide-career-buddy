Refund & Payment Support – Career Buddy

Payment and refund information for Ayesha – Your Career Buddy. Last updated: 26 October 2026.

What is charged?

Premium Career Assessment is currently ₹499, and Introductory Coaching is ₹149, before an eligible discount. Standard and Extended coaching refills are priced separately within the app. The final payable amount is shown before Razorpay confirmation.

100% discount codes

If an approved coupon reduces the payable amount to zero, no payment is collected and there is no paid amount to refund.

Access or technical problems

If a successful payment did not activate your purchased package, please contact info@aiearlycareerguide.com with your registered email and the Razorpay order or payment reference. We will investigate and seek to restore the entitlement or resolve the transaction appropriately.

Duplicate or incorrect charges

Report suspected duplicate charges promptly using your Razorpay transaction references. Refund requests will be assessed against verified payment records, whether the purchased service was delivered or used, and applicable law. Refunds, where approved, apply to the amount actually paid.

Digital-service refunds

Career Buddy is a digital service that may provide immediate access. Refund eligibility depends on the transaction circumstances, applicable consumer rights and the policy agreed at purchase. Contact support to discuss any payment or access concern.

How to contact support

Email info@aiearlycareerguide.com. Please do not send payment-card numbers, CVVs, passwords or one-time codes.
