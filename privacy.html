Privacy Policy – Career Buddy

Last updated: 31 October 2026.

Information you provide

Career Buddy processes the email address used for verification; the career background and answers you submit; assessment and report records; chat messages and paid coaching usage; and optional text submitted for resume review.

Why we use this information

We use information to authenticate accounts, save progress, provide relevant career insights, generate assessments and reports, deliver verified purchases, manage coaching credits, provide support and protect the service from misuse.

AI and service providers

Career Buddy uses Firebase for data, OpenAI for AI features, Resend for verification email, Razorpay for payment and Vercel for hosting. Submitted information necessary for a feature may be processed by the service provider involved. We do not ask for card CVVs or payment card numbers.

Optional feedback

After free reports, Premium reports or coaching responses, you may send a rating, category and optional comment. We associate feedback with your verified account and the stage or answer rated, but do not automatically copy your complete report, resume text or chat transcript into the feedback record. Authorised administrators can review, export and moderate feedback, including hiding or permanently deleting inappropriate or unclear comments. Feedback is not published as a public testimonial without separate permission.

Consent and age

Users are asked to acknowledge privacy terms and confirm they are at least 18 before verification. A separate consent step applies to optional resume review. Students should use the dedicated student experience with appropriate adult involvement.

Data minimisation

Do not submit sensitive identity numbers, bank credentials, confidential employer materials, home addresses, detailed medical information or private third-party information. Remove personal contact information from any resume before submitting it where possible.

Storage and deletion

Career-related records and reports may remain available for account continuity. We retain submitted feedback for quality review until deleted or as otherwise required by law. Moderation activity may be recorded separately without duplicating the feedback text. To request access, correction or deletion, email info@aiearlycareerguide.com from your registered address.

Service limitations

AI-generated career information is for development planning. Assessments measure responses to scenarios, not independently verified workplace performance and do not guarantee hiring outcomes.
