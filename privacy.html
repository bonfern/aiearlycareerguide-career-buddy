Privacy Policy – Career Buddy

Privacy information for Ayesha – Your Career Buddy. Last updated: 26 October 2026.

Information you provide

To operate Career Buddy we process your verified email address, career and employment details you enter, free discovery answers, paid assessment responses, optional resume text pasted into the Ayesha review tool, questions asked of the coach and reports generated.

What it is used for

We use information to verify access, save progress, personalise guidance, generate career reports and optional resume-review feedback, deliver purchases, manage coaching interactions, prevent fraud and respond to support requests.

Service providers

Career Buddy uses Firebase for application records, OpenAI to support AI-generated guidance, Resend for verification emails, Razorpay for payments and Vercel for hosting. Information needed for a feature may be processed by its service provider under the relevant agreement. We do not ask you to provide card numbers or card CVVs to Career Buddy.

What should you avoid entering?

Do not enter identification numbers, financial account credentials, full home addresses, highly sensitive health information or private employer information you are not authorised to share. You may remove personal contact details from resume text before submitting it. The review tool sends pasted text to the AI provider for analysis but does not intentionally store the original pasted resume; the resulting feedback is saved for later access.

Retention and requests

Your session and reports may remain accessible for future use, subject to the service's account and retention practices. To request access, correction or deletion, contact info@aiearlycareerguide.com from your registered email. We will review requests under applicable laws and operational requirements.

Limitations

AI-generated career advice is informational, not an independently verified assessment of your work performance or an employment guarantee.
