Career Buddy Terms & Conditions

Last updated: 31 October 2026.

What this service provides

Career Buddy provides optional professional career discovery, a paid competency assessment and AI-supported coaching for adults aged 18 or older. The free snapshot is exploratory; Premium uses role-specific multiple-choice scenarios to support learning and career planning.

Assessment and report limitations

Reports are based on your self-reported career history and scenario answers. They do not verify work performance, qualifications, identity, employability or suitability for a specific vacancy. Courses, certificates and interview suggestions are not hiring guarantees.

Paid features and credits

Pricing, discounts and credit validity are displayed before checkout. Premium includes a role-specific assessment, its report and the associated coaching pack. Resume review is a separate paid-coaching tool that deducts five credits on completed review; one completed coaching answer normally deducts one credit. Availability and terms of coupon offers may differ.

Payments

Access to paid features is granted only after verified payment or an approved zero-payable coupon. See the Refund & Payment Support policy for transaction and consumer-rights information.

Feedback rules

Feedback is optional. Please submit relevant, respectful comments without personal or sensitive data about others. Authorised administrators may hide or remove abusive, spam, offensive or unintelligible submissions. Feedback is not publicly displayed by default.

Your responsibilities

Provide information you are authorised to share, independently verify important career decisions, and do not attempt to misuse accounts, credits, automated assessments or service security.

Support

Contact info@aiearlycareerguide.com for service and policy questions. Applicable consumer rights continue to apply.
