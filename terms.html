Terms & Conditions – Career Buddy

Ayesha – Your Career Buddy. Last updated: 26 October 2026.

Service description

Career Buddy by AI Early Career Guide provides career discovery, AI-assisted coaching, professional scenario-based assessment and an optional paid-credit resume review inside coaching. Results are for guidance only; they are not employment, salary, promotion or interview guarantees.

Access and account

Use an email address you can verify. Keep your session and verification codes private. The service may limit abusive use or activity that threatens security or availability.

Free and paid offerings

Free Career Discovery generates a preliminary snapshot. Premium Career Assessment currently costs ₹499 before eligible coupons and includes a detailed report and 40 coaching credits. Intro coaching currently costs ₹149 before eligible coupons and includes 12 credits valid for 14 days. Current refill options are shown in the service. Prices shown at checkout determine the final charge.

Credits and usage

Coaching packs grant a defined number of interactions and validity period. An interaction is consumed when Career Buddy answers a submitted question; technical failures are not intended to consume an interaction. The detailed Premium assessment questions themselves do not spend coaching credits. A completed standalone Ayesha resume review uses five credits and provides downloadable feedback, not a rewritten resume.

Payments and coupons

Payments are processed through Razorpay. Coupons apply only according to the eligibility and limits displayed or configured for the promotion. A successfully verified payment or authorised zero-payable coupon is needed before paid access is granted.

Your responsibilities

Supply truthful, appropriate information; avoid disclosing third-party confidential data; and independently verify all generated guidance, learning options, interview suggestions and resume-review advice before making decisions.

Support and refunds

For billing, access or service issues, contact info@aiearlycareerguide.com. See the Career Buddy Refund Policy for the support process. Mandatory consumer protections remain applicable.
