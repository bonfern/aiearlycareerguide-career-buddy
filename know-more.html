Know More

Career Buddy is the professional career-guidance experience by AI Early Career Guide, created for people exploring their next role, promotion or career change.

How it works

  1. Free Career Discovery: verify your email and complete seven short questions about your current role, career goal and skills you have used. Review and change answers before generating your snapshot.
  2. Free career snapshot: receive a preliminary interpretation and an overview of competencies relevant to your target role. This is not a tested competency score.
  3. Choose your next step: purchase the Premium Career Assessment or the introductory Career Buddy Coaching pack if you want deeper support.
  4. Premium: answer role-specific scenario questions, then receive a detailed development report with competency gaps, learning actions and interview preparation.
  5. Coaching: ask personalised career questions. Each successfully answered question uses one credit; a separate optional resume review uses five credits.

What the free snapshot does not include

The free snapshot does not test your technical knowledge, verify employment history or produce a scored skills-gap diagnosis. These require the separately purchased Premium Assessment.
