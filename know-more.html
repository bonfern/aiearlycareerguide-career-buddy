Know More

Explore your next professional move with a free career snapshot, an optional role-specific Premium Assessment and personalised Career Buddy coaching.

How the journey works

  1. Verify your email and start free discovery. Answer seven focused questions about career stage, priorities, strengths and direction, with an additional interest question when relevant.
  2. Get your free snapshot. Read your profile, transferable strengths, four important skills (with explanations), three possible career pathways and three immediate exploration actions. These are exploratory insights, not skill-test results.
  3. Choose Premium or coaching. Both are optional and follow the free snapshot.
  4. Premium Career Assessment. Take 12–15 role-specific scenario and knowledge questions across four or five key competencies; your completed assessment generates a detailed development report.
  5. Career Buddy coaching. Ask follow-up questions using your saved career context. Completed coaching answers use credits; optional resume review is five credits.

What Premium evaluates

Premium evaluates the judgement shown in your answers against target-role technical and behavioural competencies. It does not independently verify past workplace performance.

What your Premium report includes

  • Career goal and summary, essential role competencies and their benchmarks.
  • Technical, behavioural and leadership priorities, skill-by-skill results and untested areas.
  • Practical development actions, subskills to practise, evidence tasks and success indicators.
  • Career progression actions and a personalised 30/60/90-day execution plan.
  • Learning resources, optional courses and certifications, and progress checklist.
  • Role-specific interview questions and answer-preparation frameworks.
  • 40 Career Buddy coaching credits valid for the duration shown at checkout.
