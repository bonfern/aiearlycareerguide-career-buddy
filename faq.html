Frequently Asked Questions

Is Career Discovery really free?

Yes. Verified-email users can complete the guided discovery questions and receive a preliminary downloadable snapshot without payment.

Can I change an answer?

Yes. Before generating the free snapshot, select Previous or Review answers. Revisions do not make additional OpenAI requests. After a snapshot has been saved, the completed free assessment is not reopened for editing.

Is the free snapshot a test of my skills?

No. It interprets what you report about yourself and describes relevant target-role skills. It does not grade competence or promise a job outcome.

What does Premium include?

Premium adds a role-specific 12–15 question scenario assessment, a detailed career-development report, practical learning and interview preparation, optional resume revision and 60 coaching interactions.

What does the coaching-only plan include?

The introductory ₹149 plan provides 15 Career Buddy interactions for 14 days, drawing on your free snapshot. It does not include the Premium competency assessment.

Will my answers be saved?

Verified-email sessions save your progress and available reports so you can continue with the same account.

How are coupons applied?

Enter a valid code at the paid-plan screen. You will see the final payable amount before confirming a Razorpay payment. A valid full-discount coupon may activate access without a charge.

Can I use my resume?

Yes. Premium users can optionally paste resume text for a suggested revision. Review all generated details before using any resume for applications.

How can I contact support?

Email info@aiearlycareerguide.com. Never send passwords or payment-card details.
