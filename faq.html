Frequently asked questions

Is career discovery free?

Yes. Eligible verified-email users aged 18 or over can complete the short free discovery and download a snapshot without payment.

How many discovery questions?

Seven focused questions, plus an interest question when you are exploring different directions. This is separate from the Premium Assessment.

Can I change my free discovery answers?

Before generating the free snapshot, you can go back to review or change your answers. A saved snapshot is preserved; completed assessments are not reopened for editing.

What does Premium include?

Premium includes 12–15 role-specific questions across four or five competencies, a detailed report of assessed skills and growth priorities, practical development actions, a 30/60/90-day plan, learning suggestions, interview preparation and 40 coaching credits.

Can I see an example first?

Yes. View the fictional sample Premium Assessment report. It shows the format, not guaranteed results for a particular user.

Does Premium verify job performance?

No. It evaluates responses to role-specific scenarios and self-reported background. The report explains that results should be supported with real-world evidence.

How long do reports take?

Question preparation and detailed report generation commonly take around 2–3 minutes each and can take longer. The app displays preparation stages and processing status.

What about resume review?

Resume review is a separate Career Buddy coaching tool, available with eligible paid coaching access at five credits per completed review. It provides feedback, not an automatically rewritten resume.

How is feedback collected?

You can optionally rate the Free Snapshot, Premium Report or individual Career Buddy replies. Comments are reviewed privately by authorised administrators and may be hidden or deleted when irrelevant or offensive.

Will my progress be saved?

Yes, assessment answers and reports are saved under your verified email, so you can resume using the same account.

How do payments and coupons work?

The checkout screen shows the payable amount after an eligible coupon; access is granted after verified payment or an authorised zero-payable coupon.

How can I get help?

Email info@aiearlycareerguide.com with your registered email and a description of the issue. Never share one-time codes or card details.
